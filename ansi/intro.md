Jason Voorhees: Look for "NES Friday the 13th Jason sprite". The purple/blue
palette pops really well in terminals.

Freddy: "A Nightmare on Elm Street NES sprite" (orange/red stripes are high
contrast).

Pennywise: Look for "GBA" or "SNES" style sprites, as they have more detail than
8-bit but aren't photo-realistic (which looks messy in ANSI).
